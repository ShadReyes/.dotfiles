# Etsy listing guidance

Checked 2026-10-11. Shared reference for decalflow skills. Etsy sources govern platform rules; the production defaults below are local choices. Recheck the linked Etsy pages when publishing or when platform behavior changes.

## Size, orientation, and export

[Etsy's image guidance](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115015663347-Requirements-and-Best-Practices-for-Images-in-Your-Etsy-Shop) recommends:

- At least **2000 px in both dimensions**. Its separate first-photo search threshold is 635 px in both dimensions; that is not a quality target.
- A landscape or square first image. The same page later advises landscape uploads and avoiding square crops. Prefer landscape for new images; square remains an accepted recommendation, not a universal requirement.
- Similar image shapes throughout a listing, with the subject central and enough space for square, portrait, and landscape thumbnails. Preview with Etsy's thumbnail tool.
- sRGB color. JPG and PNG are supported; transparent PNG regions can appear black.
- Files over 1 MB can have upload problems, especially on slow connections. This is upload guidance, not a stated hard file-size limit.

**Local default:** use **3000 × 2250 px (4:3 landscape)** for new photographic listing images. Use **2400 × 2400 px** when a square composition works better. Neither dimension pair is an Etsy-mandated format or a proven conversion winner. Export photos as high-quality JPG and text-heavy graphics as opaque PNG when that preserves legibility. Compress without blurring fine lettering; increasing pixel dimensions cannot restore missing detail.

Preserve approved template dimensions and shared graphics unless a redesign is requested. A portrait size or installation guide can remain a supporting image. Listing-image pixels do not set the physical dimensions or aspect ratio of a cut-ready SVG/PDF.

## Focus and useful image roles

[Etsy's photo guide](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/22504064051) recommends a clear first photo and supporting views that explain use, scale, details, variations, or packaging. [Shopify's photography guide](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/product-media/product-photography) likewise advises removing visual distractions. [Baymard's usability research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/in-scale-product-images) supports showing products in a familiar scale context; its [resolution research](https://baymard.com/research-articles/ensure-sufficient-image-resolution-and-zoom) supports sharp details that buyers can inspect. These findings support clarity and buyer confidence, not a guaranteed conversion increase.

Apply that guidance to decals:

- **First image:** one clear finished design. Make the lettering prominent, with contrast against the surface. Keep reflections, flowers, and props from competing with it. Put detailed instructions in supporting images.
- **Supporting images:** answer the useful questions: what the detail looks like, how large it is, what ships, and how to personalize or install it. Reuse the approved About, Size Guide, and Installation graphics; add views only when they add information. Keep mirror-not-included information clear.
- **Crop check:** inspect full resolution and roughly 300 px wide, then test square, portrait, and landscape crops. Keep the complete design recognizable with breathing room. Adjust framing instead of changing letter shapes or stretching artwork. There is no universal safe-margin percentage.

## Actual photos and mockups

[Etsy's listing-image policy](https://www.etsy.com/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005) generally requires original photos of the actual product. For personalized items, the first image must show a real finished customized example; a blank product or placeholder-text mockup is not suitable. Computer-generated mockups may show customization options in additional images. Production-partner exceptions are specific; do not assume they cover a personalized decal.

For this workflow, keep generated lifestyle composites in supporting roles. If a suitable real first photo is absent, finish the requested design/mockup work and report that missing upload asset. A mockup-only delivery can be complete as a mockup task, but not as an Etsy-ready full listing set. Competitor photos remain review references, not customer upload assets.

## Competitor evidence

Record source URLs and distinguish item reviews, Bestseller badges, cart activity, and shop sales. None establishes exact item sales or conversion rate. State whether a ranking compares individual listings or grouped product types; preserve the requested comparison scope. Treat a successful-looking composition as a reference, not proof that its crop or image size caused sales.
