# Research with Etsy Marketplace Insights

Use this procedure for live research or interpretation of saved Marketplace Insights results. The current interface is authoritative for available controls, periods, definitions, and limits.

## Capture comparable evidence

Use the connected first-party interface when available. For authorized browser work, load `cua-driver` and its platform/browser instructions; keep one UI writer for the target tab. Follow that skill's binding, snapshot, input, and verification rules. An existing Chrome login does not by itself grant DevTools access. A connection failure leaves uncaptured queries pending; do not convert it into a zero-demand observation.

Cover the research scope with product/material/surface terms, intended use, exact wording, broader comparators, and supported custom/personalized variants. Include existing tags when auditing a listing. Explore related terms, then directly search promising candidates. For a broad audit, cover these dimensions before expanding into minor wording variants; a narrow tag edit can use a smaller comparison set.

For each observed query, retain:

- Exact submitted phrase, displayed phrase, source URL, capture timestamp/timezone, displayed period, and any stated search geography. Interface locale or currency does not establish the population searched.
- Displayed searches, competing results, conversion label and its definition, plus trend information when used. Preserve rounded `k`/`M` text alongside any approximate numeric expansion.
- Whether the values came from a direct query or a related-term row, the originating query, and the screenshot or accessibility snapshot that verifies them.
- Whether content settled, remained loading, showed unavailable values, or was not queried. A related table still loading is incomplete evidence even when the headline metrics are ready.

Wait for the intended query's results and inspect the rendered page. Cross-check the shortlist against retained evidence before using it in a tag plan. Record screenshot-only transcription explicitly when accessibility data are unavailable. Direct totals and related-row counts can differ; preserve their provenance rather than replacing one with the other.

## Interpret the signals

The October 22, 2026 interface displayed `Very low`, `Low`, `Typical`, `High`, and `Very high` conversion labels. Read the live explanatory text before relying on those labels. They describe marketplace purchase frequency relative to what is typical on Etsy, not a shop's conversion rate. Without displayed percentages, keep them as ordered categories; two `Very high` phrases have no established exact conversion ordering.

Assess truthful intent first, then demand, conversion tier, competing results, and distinct coverage together. Broad phrases can have much greater volume while matching a different product or facing far more competition. A narrow term with a favorable label can still have little demand. A search-to-results ratio does not measure attainable visits, competitive quality, or conversion.

Treat dashes, missing values, and uncaptured queries as unknown, not zero. Keep low-count trends in context; a large percentage change from a small base does not establish substantial demand. A short seasonal snapshot does not prove annual performance, and marketplace observations do not establish that a tag caused sales.

Research queries can exceed 20 characters; final tags cannot. Use a meaningful shorter phrase only when its own wording fits the product. Splitting a long query across tags does not guarantee the original exact-phrase match.

## Retained local research

The October 22 research covered 37 submitted queries plus one preexisting open query, with 216 related-term observations. Independent review checked all 38 headline observations without mismatches. Its displayed 30-day chart covered September 22–October 21, 2026. One late result was visually transcribed after Chrome's accessibility connection failed; three additional optional queries were not captured.

On the originating machine, the evidence remains under `~/.dotfiles/local-runtime/etsy-keywords/2026-10-22/`: `report.html`, `results.json`, `keywords.csv`, `independent-review.json`, `capture-limitations.json`, and retained snapshots. Consult it when revisiting that run; if absent, use new research rather than inventing the old measurements. Keep authenticated screenshots, private shop data, and detailed run records outside public skill files.

The revised [conditional base](wedding-base.md) carries the reusable selection decisions. Its candidates are dated starting points; refresh their evidence for later launches rather than treating this snapshot as permanent demand or conversion proof.
