## Product-specific slots

Select from verified facts: actual wording, function (seating, welcome, memorial, photo sign), surface, visual style, supported customization, and useful regional phrases. Cover distinct intent rather than filling nine slots with small changes to one phrase. Personalization is a product-specific choice, never part of the fixed base. Sample names and dates are not keywords.

For a fixed “Your Seat Awaits” heading, the research supports these product-specific candidates. This is a shortlist, not a complete nine-tag allocation:

| Candidate | Characters | Selection reason |
|---|---:|---|
| your seat awaits | 16 | Actual headline wording |
| seating chart decal | 19 | Direct decal-shopping intent; keep heading-only contents explicit in the listing |
| seating chart vinyl | 19 | Alternative material-qualified seating phrase; compare its incremental coverage with seating chart decal |

Make heading-only contents clear in the title, first image, and opening description. Precise component language belongs there even when it is a weak search tag. `seating chart header` and `table plan header` had weak observed demand and conversion labels. `seating plan heading`, `script sign sticker`, and `chart heading decal` had unavailable measurements; that is not evidence of zero demand. Remove them from automatic example tags and re-evaluate them only with relevant evidence. Longer queries such as `wedding mirror sticker` (22 characters) and `wedding seating chart decal` (27) can inform copy but cannot be pasted as single tags.

Exact wording can have small but relevant demand. Alternative quotations such as “Find Your Seat” can attract buyers expecting those literal words. A phrase such as “Smile You're on Camera” can lead toward security-sign intent; inspect the related terms and actual result types before treating it as a wedding keyword. High search volume cannot justify guest-name charts, a finished mirror, or wording the product does not supply.

## Custom and personalized offers

For supported wording or name/date customization, research `custom mirror decal` (19 characters) and `custom vinyl decal` (18) as conditional candidates. The October snapshot favored the more focused mirror phrase over `custom wedding decal`; the broader vinyl phrase had greater demand and much more competition. Compare `custom` and `personalized` wording instead of assuming they have equal demand. Made-to-order manufacture, size choices, and color options alone do not establish wording customization. Keep these tags off the fixed heading listing.

For a welcome design, replace seating/quote tags with its actual wording and welcome use. For a non-wedding or board-only decal, replace wedding or mirror tags respectively. Recheck the snapshot as seasons and buyer behavior change; do not copy this shortlist unchanged into unrelated launches.
